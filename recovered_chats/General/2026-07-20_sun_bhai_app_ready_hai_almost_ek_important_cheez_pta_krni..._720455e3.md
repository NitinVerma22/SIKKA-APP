### 4. Final Recommendation & Strategic Advice

Based on the math, **I strongly recommend using the 1000 Coins = ₹1 ratio** (assuming you give out ~1000 coins a day). 

Here is why this is the perfect economy for India:

#### 1. It is Actually Profitable (36% Gross Margin)
At 1000 = ₹1, the user earns **₹1.00 per day** for 15 minutes of gameplay. 
You earn **₹1.57 per day**. 
Your margin is **₹0.57 per user/day**. This gives you the budget to pay for Firebase/AWS server costs, UPI transaction fees, and a robust Referral Program (e.g., "Invite a friend, get ₹2 / 2000 Coins"). 

#### 2. The Psychology of Big Numbers
Users in the 15–22 demographic feel much more rewarded when they see their balance jump by "+85 Coins" from a Gullak rather than "+8 Coins". Using a 1000:1 ratio allows you to give out hundreds of coins per session without going bankrupt.

#### 3. Preventing Abuse and Fraud
If you offer 100 Coins = ₹1 (paying out ₹10/day), hackers and emulator bots will swarm your app immediately to drain your funds. At ₹1.00 a day, the payout is attractive enough for real students/teens looking for pocket money, but too low to be worth the server resources for massive bot farms.

#### 4. The Withdrawal Strategy
Set your Minimum Withdrawal to **₹10 (10,000 Coins)** via Paytm/UPI. 
*   **Why?** Earning 1,000 coins a day means it will take the user exactly **10 days** to reach the withdrawal limit. 
*   This artificially boosts your **Day-7 and Day-14 Retention rates**, which tells AdMob and the Google Play algorithm that your app is high quality, eventually leading to better organic traffic and higher ad eCPMs.

#### 5. Dynamic Reward Balancing (Crucial)
Do not give fixed rewards for the Gullak (e.g., exactly 80 coins). Use a randomized range (e.g., 65 to 110 coins, mathematically averaging out to 80). The "slot machine effect" of randomized rewards releases more dopamine and keeps users playing the mini-games longer in hopes of hitting a "100+ coin" Gullak.
